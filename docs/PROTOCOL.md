# Field validation protocol

1. **Frame and photo:** 0.5 x 0.5 m frame, camera straight down at about 1 m, diffuse light, include a grey card; fixed white balance if possible.
2. **Reference:** within minutes, in the same frame, two observers estimate cover independently; then cut at the standard stubble height, separate grass, legume, forb and senescent by hand, dry at 60 C and weigh.
3. **Sampling:** at least 30 quadrats per sward type, across at least 3 sites and several regrowths; include pure grass, pure legume, flowering, lodged and senescent canopies.
4. **Split by site** for calibration and testing.
5. **Report** camera, light, sample sizes, every failure, and negative results.
